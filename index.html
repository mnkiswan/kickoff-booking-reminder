03 — THE PROBLEM

Clients forgot their kickoff

Without a reminder, kickoffs were missed or pushed back, which slowed onboarding for everyone.

04 — THE BUSINESS IMPACT

Why this workflow is worth building

Fewer

Missed kickoffs

Clients get the reminder without anyone remembering to send it.

Right

Message per case

The decision step sends the reminder that fits the contact.

Less

Chasing clients

Staff stop sending confirmations by hand.
