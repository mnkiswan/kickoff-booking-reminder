The problem

Clients forgot their kickoff

Without a reminder, clients missed or forgot kickoff calls, which pushed onboarding back.

How it works

From trigger to finished work

Trigger: a kickoff form entry is submitted.

01

Form submitted

A client submits the kickoff form.

02

Wait

A delay step holds the flow before the next action.

03

Look up the contact

The contact is fetched from Zoho CRM.

04

Decide which reminder to send

A decision step checks the record. If no condition matches, the Default branch runs.

05

Send the email template

The matched branch sends the reminder email.

Why it matters for the business

What changes when this runs

Fewer missed kickoffs

Clients get a reminder without anyone remembering to send one.

Right message for each case

The decision step lets the reminder match the situation.

Less follow-up work

Staff do not chase clients for confirmations.
